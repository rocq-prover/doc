Chapter 11: The tactic language

This chapter gives a compact documentation of the tactic language available in the toplevel of Coq. We start by giving the syntax and, next, we present the informal semantic. Finally, we show some examples which deal with small but also with non-trivial problems. If you want to know more regarding this language and especially about its fundations, you can refer to [Del00].
