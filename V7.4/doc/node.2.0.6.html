10.7 Summary of the options of Notation

The different syntactic variants of the command Notation are the following
  • Notation string := term.

  • Notation ident := term.

  • Notation string := term ( modifier , ... , modifier ).
where a modifier is one of at level n, no associativity, right associativity, left associativity, only parsing or ident , ... , ident at level n,

Notations do not survive the end of sections. No typing of the denoted expression is performed at definition time. Type-checking is done only at the time of use of the notation.
